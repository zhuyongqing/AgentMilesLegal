2. What the app does

Agent Miles is a mileage log built for US real estate agents. It detects and records drives, lets you classify each one by category and activity, tags drives to clients or properties, prices each drive at the IRS standard mileage rate in force on the day it was driven, and produces a PDF or CSV report you can hand to a tax professional.

Deductibility depends on your own circumstances and current IRS rules — confirm anything you file with a qualified tax professional.

Mileage rates and totals are calculated from the information in the app. If you enter a drive incorrectly, or classify it incorrectly, the report will be wrong, and the report is only as good as the data in it.
