Agent Miles

Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 October 2026

The short version

Agent Miles has no account, no server and no analytics. Your driving record — including client addresses and every route you take — is stored only on this device. Nothing is sent to us, because there is nowhere for it to go.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, transmit, sell or have access to any of your data. The app does not create an account and does not talk to a backend of ours.

What the app stores on your device

  • Location and motion data. While a drive is being recorded, the app saves the route, start and end coordinates, timestamps and GPS accuracy, so it can log the drive and price it for tax purposes.
  • Drive details you enter. Category, activity, notes, parking and toll amounts, odometer readings, and any client or property tags you add.
  • Receipt photos. If you attach a photo of a parking ticket or toll receipt, the image is resized and stored with that drive. The app uses the system photo picker, which gives it access only to the photos you select. Agent Miles cannot browse your photo library and does not ask for permission to.
  • Preferences. Distance unit, work hours, notification settings and similar choices.

All of it lives inside the app's own storage on this device.

What leaves your device

Only what you explicitly export, and only when you choose to:

  • CSV and PDF reports are written to a temporary file and handed to the standard iOS share sheet. Where they go after that — email, Files, AirDrop — is your choice.
  • JSON backups work the same way. A backup contains your drives, vehicles, clients, properties and receipt photos, and it is written wherever you send it. Keep it somewhere you consider safe.

What we do not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no cloud sync.
  • No analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, no advertising SDK.
  • No tracking of any kind, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party code that receives your data.
  • No selling or sharing of data — we never have it to sell.

Permissions the app asks for

Location. Drive detection has to keep running while the app is in the background, which is why the app asks for "Always". You can choose "While Using the App" instead: detection then only runs with the app open, and manual entry is unaffected. You can change or revoke this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Agent Miles.

Motion & Fitness. Used to tell driving apart from walking so the app does not log a trip to the mailbox. Processed on device; used for nothing else.

Notifications. Used to tell you a drive was recorded and that there are drives waiting to be classified. Optional.

Maps and addresses

Drawing a map and turning coordinates into a street address is handled by Apple's MapKit and geocoding services, so those requests go to Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not to us. We never see them.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete All Data removes every drive, vehicle, client, property, saved place and receipt photo from the app immediately and permanently. Deleting the app also deletes everything, because nothing is stored anywhere else.

We cannot restore your data for you, because we never had it. That is exactly why the app offers a backup file that you control — please use it.

Children

Agent Miles is a business tool and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If a future version ever changes any of the above — for example by adding optional crash reporting — this page will be updated before that version ships, and the App Store privacy label will change with it.
